## Who starts a Checker, and when

**The seat one layer up from the seat packing out (its manager) starts it, by command, the moment the seat says "ready for review".** That's the seat's Admiral, its VP, or the session that spun it. **A seat with nobody above it (The VP, a Fleet Admiral) starts its own Checker, and names Justin as its escalation contact.**

**Why one layer up, and not the seat itself:**
- **Independence:** the seat doesn't choose who checks it.
- **You're the Checker's first escalation contact,** so you're the session that needs to know it exists.
- **A session started by command survives** and can write to the Log. A subagent's output would land in your context and die with your session.

**One Checker per packout. It checks, posts its verdict, messages you, and stops; then you stop its session.** **Confirm its verdict message in one line, and don't reply again after that.**

**The Checker does NOT verify the successor's load.** That stays with whoever launches the successor, exactly as before.

## The launch command

**Run it from the `_evryn-meta` folder, irrespective of what folder you're in.** A session starts in the folder its command runs from, and only `_evryn-meta` hands it the router that routes it to its definition:
```
cd <shared parent>/_evryn-meta && claude --bg --agent packout-checker -n "Packout Checker <seat name>" "<the start line below>"
```
The agent definition pins its model (Opus) and removes its editing tools.
