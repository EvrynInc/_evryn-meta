## §2 — THE QUESTION, AND WHAT JUSTIN ASKED FOR

**The one question (2026-09-14):** *Should I keep building the autonomous agents in the Claude Agent SDK, or is another direction better?*

**Why he is asking — two converging forces:** the autonomy build is getting very complex and he would have to build AND maintain it; and Claude Code keeps gaining capabilities, the team already works in it, and it is getting more autonomous — so he may be *"trying to fix what's becoming less and less broken every day."*

**His sub-questions, in his order:**

1. **Does Claude Code offer the functionality he needs?** — **post-compaction reload** instead of every agent stopping *(his estimate: ~48% of what SDK autonomy was for)* · **heartbeat / scheduled wakeups** so agents work round the clock *(~48%)*, richer than today's, with hardwired on-wake checks · **instance-to-instance messaging** *(~2%)* · Slack from his phone into a cloud session · **cloud-native**: reachable from desktop, laptop and phone without living on any of them.
2. **Sessions.** The SDK build avoided them because compaction summaries are bad. A hook saying *"treat the summary as just okay — reload your working doc"* might make sessions fine — which would simplify the SDK build too.
3. **Mobile** — agents must not stall when he is away from his desk. Has Remote Control solved it?
4. **Outages** — he dislikes fearing a power or internet cut. The Railway runtime has 100% uptime; the Claude Code team lives on his desktop. **What is the cloud-native option?**
5. **The coding system prompt** — how much context does Claude Code's software-engineering prompt waste for non-coding agents, and does it fight his agents? With our own harness we control that; with Claude Code every update may bring new under-the-hood behavior.
6. **Anthropic Managed Agents** — a halfway point?
7. **Third-party harnesses** — he prefers staying in the Anthropic ecosystem but wants the options considered.
8. **Prior research exists** and the balance of forces may have moved.

**He also named Cowork** *(verbatim: "maybe sometimes using Cowork for certain types of functionality?")* ⇒ **Cowork is a candidate surface alongside Claude Code.**

**His calibrations, verbatim:** *"for much of this, you having a 10,000-ft view will be enough"* · *"So I wonder if I shouldn't be trying to fix what's becoming less and less broken every day. ### 3.4 · This environment, first-hand `[R]` — read from the session's own tools

- **Cross-session messaging works; subagent-to-subagent does not.** A subagent's messages go out under its parent's address and replies return to the parent, **so two subagents of one session cannot converse** — separate sessions can.
- **In-session scheduling (`CronCreate`, the `/loop` substrate) dies with the session** — *"nothing is written to disk"*, `durable` has no effect, jobs fire only while the REPL is idle, recurring jobs auto-expire after 7 days. **`Monitor` is likewise session-scoped.**

- **`PushNotification`** reaches his phone **only if Remote Control is connected.**
- 🆕 **`/clear` does NOT clear a session in place — it starts a NEW session and leaves the old one running.** *(Observed 2026-09-17. This corrects the previous handoff, which treated `/clear` as an in-place wipe and left the transcript question open.)* ⇒ **For a daytime re-spin, `/clear` gives you a fresh instance while the old session and its transcript stay alive.** ⚠️ **Whether its SUBAGENTS stay reachable from the new session is NOT established here** — §6 measured resume across a **compaction**, not across a `/clear`. **Justin authorized that follow-up test on 2026-09-17; record its result in §6 when it runs.**
