### 5.4 · The finding shape

```
**Pa-01 · MISLEADING | MISSING | UNDER-RESOLVED | STALE (· low) · <short title>**
- **Evidence:** `L<n>` (or a file, or a commit) — quote the words that carry it
- **Handoff now:** <section> — quote it, or "absent"
- **Why it matters:** what a fresh ACT-A would do wrong, or fail to do, without the fix
- **Proposed fix:** the exact text, and where it goes
```

- **The severities:**
  - **MISLEADING** — a fresh instance would act wrongly.
  - **MISSING** — it would lack something it needs.
  - **UNDER-RESOLVED** — present, but compressed past usefulness.
  - **STALE** — true once, false now.
  - **Add "low"** where the consequence is small.
- **Numbering:** `Pa-NN`, `Pb-NN`, `Pc-NN`.
- **Pc settles each finding, one of three ways:** `landed at §x (<commit>)` · `rejected because …` · `for Justin: …`.
