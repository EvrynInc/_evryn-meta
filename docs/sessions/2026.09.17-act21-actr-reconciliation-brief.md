**Evryn's team-runtime work leaves a trail of session documents** — handoffs, lane briefs, exchange logs, set-downs. ⚠️ **Output 2 without output 1 is the failure mode, not the job.** **Archiving a document that was the sole home of a live finding destroys the finding and makes the destruction invisible.** **Never retire a doc you have not read.**
