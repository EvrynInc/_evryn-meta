### §14.6 — Lessons from running transcript readers

*These are the generalizable halves, for `LEARNINGS.md` once their local halves land (lock step 5e).*
- **Loading §2 costs a sub-AC about 470K.** That is with a general-purpose spawn, which reads `ac.md` once. An `ac`-type spawn carries the manual twice, about 76K more.

- **A transcript stores reasoning only as summaries.** **Anything delivered while the agent was mid-turn arrives through the queue** (`queue-operation` records and `queued_command` attachments). A render or a reader that skips the queue misses Justin's mid-turn messages and every subagent report. ⚠️ **ACT-19's lean-tail render also dropped reasoning entries**, a defect ACT-19c caught.
- **A background subagent's tool call can hang silently.** Only a running heartbeat, or a stall watch on the subagent's transcript modification time, will notice. `TaskStop` can leave the agent "still stopping" for minutes, and a resume works once it exits.
