**ACfm's Step-4 spec §2.5 would have silently killed `/resume`'s parked-work re-arm.** The spec said that removing the scheduler's addressed-message scan was *"a confirmation rather than a change"* because park notes are written inert. **That is true of the automatic path and false of the deliberate one.**
