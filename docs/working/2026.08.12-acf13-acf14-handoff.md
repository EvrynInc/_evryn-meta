## §6 — 🔴 THE LESSONS FROM THIS ROUND THAT ARE NOT YET IN ANY MANUAL

**These are the durable output of the last two days and they currently live only here. That is the "recorded but never delivered" failure waiting to happen — promote them.**

1. ⭐ **A GUARD MUST BE PROVEN IN BOTH DIRECTIONS: it fires on bad input AND stays silent on good input.** We have a well-developed discipline for the first half (*"watch it fail against deliberately-broken real code"*) and **essentially none for the second.**
   **FOUR instruments produced false results in one night and EVERY ONE was caught by a control, not by care:** a merge-gate script that reported RED on known-good code (a multi-byte `ℹ` broke its parse); a builder's token-grep applied-proof that matched **a comment quoting the mutated string**; a reviewer's mutation harness that spawned a Windows shim `execFileSync` cannot exec, so **all 24 mutations threw, were caught, and were recorded as RED** — caught only because a control mutation changing one comment word *also* reported RED; and CRLF silently voiding hand-typed multi-line find-strings, on two separate agents.
   **⇒ A false-RED is the SAFE direction, which is exactly why it survives review: it never causes an incident, it just quietly destroys trust in the instrument.**
2. **"Decorative" and "real but BLIND" are DIFFERENT failure classes and collapsing them costs you.** A guard that asserts nothing versus a guard that genuinely works over the wrong scope. **The second looks healthy from the inside**, and only its *scope* tells you which you have.
3. **The failure class reproduces inside its own cure.** A header that deletes a false blanket claim and makes a new one three lines later; a lane whose headline finding was an unfalsifiable assertion shipping a fresh one. **Expect it; check the fix for the disease.**
4. **The auto-injected `CLAUDE.md` is a SNAPSHOT FROM SESSION START and does not track later edits.** **Three independent confirmations.** Every subagent inherits its spinner's snapshot, so **a whole tree can run on a stale manual while each believes it auto-loaded.** *(A fourth instance: `ACfsq`'s copy of ADR-052 went stale mid-session under my own edit.)*
5. **`#cascade-override` costs you the omission-catcher, not just the reconcile.** A DC without the token caught an identity-half omission in its brief *because* its reconcile still ran. **The token that swaps the load also disables the one step that catches the SPINNER's mistake.**
6. **A lane AC's account of what it has NOT received is unreliable.** When a lane is stopped, its child's completion can land on the **grandparent** instead. One lane reported *"no independent review exists"* while I was holding its reviewer's full GO. **Verify by artifact.**
7. **`git merge-base --is-ancestor X main` is TRUE both when X is merged AND when X is empty** — it cannot distinguish *done* from *not started*. **Two agents hit this independently in one night.** Use `git rev-list --count main..branch`.
8. **`git commit -- <path>` silently NO-OPS on an untracked file** — and in an `&&` chain the later command still prints success. **`git add` first.**
9. **Provision worktrees with a real `npm ci`, never a junction.** Six reaps, `node_modules` 146 → 146, zero incidents.
