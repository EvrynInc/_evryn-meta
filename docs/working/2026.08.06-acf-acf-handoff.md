- **⭐ Reading cannot find an ABSENCE.** Every serious finding this week was a missing thing — a missing row limit, a missing alarm, a missing ability to read a file. **You only find them by breaking something and watching whether anything complains.** This is now the lane's standing method.

- **Verify against the artifact, never the narrative.** A sub-AC's reported tip **lagged its real tip** by one commit — and that commit was unreviewed by its own QC. Check the ref, not the report.
- **This runtime punishes local reasoning almost perfectly** — behavior is half `src/` and half the identity files; defects live in seams several hops from the changed line.
