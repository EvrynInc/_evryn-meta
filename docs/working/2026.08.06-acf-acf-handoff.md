## §6 — LESSONS — don't re-pay these

- **⭐ Reading cannot find an ABSENCE.** Every serious finding this week was a missing thing — a missing row limit, a missing alarm, a missing ability to read a file. **You only find them by breaking something and watching whether anything complains.** This is now the lane's standing method.
- **⭐ A review layer earns its cost only if it attacks DIFFERENTLY from the layer above.** Across two AC-under-AC runs, **every best catch came from BELOW the top agent, and both times the top agent had already checked the thing that was wrong and passed it.** The sharpest: a guard passed one lane's mutation and failed its reviewer's — **the difference was a trailing comment.** Never let a reviewer inherit the author's test inputs. *(In `AGENT_PATTERNS.md`.)*
- **⭐ "I'll keep going with X" then stopping.** The pull to end a turn is strongest right after a long, complete-*feeling* message — strongest of all when it ends in a question. **⇒ Do the work FIRST, report in past tense.** A turn ends when you emit text and make no further tool call; text alone does not end it, so you *can* answer inline and keep working.
- **Verify against the artifact, never the narrative.** A sub-AC's reported tip **lagged its real tip** by one commit — and that commit was unreviewed by its own QC. Check the ref, not the report.
- **This runtime punishes local reasoning almost perfectly** — behavior is half `src/` and half the identity files; defects live in seams several hops from the changed line.
- **`main` moves under a lane.** Rebase rather than merge-into, and use `git diff main...branch` (**three dots**) — two-dot renders anything `main` gained as a *deletion by your branch*, which reads as though the lane reverted someone's work.
- **A binary file defeats GNU `diff`, not just git** — when git says *Binary files differ*, your whole text-tooling layer is blind.
- **Never junction `node_modules` into a mutation sandbox** — removing the junction took the live worktree's `node_modules` with it. Give each sandbox its own install.
