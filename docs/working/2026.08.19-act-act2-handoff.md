## §8 — LESSONS THAT COST US, KEPT BECAUSE THEY WILL RECUR

1. 🔴 **A GREP CANNOT PROVE ABSENCE, and absence is the answer most of your important questions take.** *"Is there another call site?"* is a completeness claim, and a clean grep **feels** like a result while meaning only *"my pattern did not match."* ⚠️ **Two independent greps once both reported "two" copies of a test helper; reading the lines found seven.** ⇒ **When two searches disagree, STOP AND READ — do not write a third pattern.** *(Now in `ac.md`.)*
2. 🔴 **NOTHING IS TRUE UNTIL YOU RUN IT.** *(Justin, on a QC's well-argued read: "she only READ.")* **A well-evidenced chain is not a measurement.** Separate what you PROVED from what you ARGUED, link by link, and say which is which.
3. 🔴 **VERIFY THE CONTENT, NOT THE EXIT CODE.** A mailbox entry shipped with its apostrophes mangled by a shell-escaping bug — the write succeeded, the commit succeeded, the content was wrong. **Same class as a breadcrumb that shipped with its paths stripped to nothing.** ⇒ **Read back what you wrote.** *(And prefer the visible Read/Write/Edit tools for documents — Justin's standing preference, and this is why.)*
