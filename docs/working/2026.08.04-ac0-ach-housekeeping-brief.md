## §1 — THE EDGE-CASE LIST IS AS VALUABLE AS THE MAIN LIST

Read this before you start, because it inverts the instinct you will otherwise have.

You will feel pressure to classify everything — to produce a clean, complete, confident list. **Resist it.** The items you *cannot* confidently classify are precisely where a wrong call would cost something real, and surfacing them honestly is worth more to us than a tidy list that quietly guesses on three of them.
